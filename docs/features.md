Status values: **Planned**, **In progress**, **Implemented** (code exists), **Verified** (tests and a real `ssh` run). Rows say what is true now: M0 and M1 rows are Implemented or Verified; M2 rows are Verified where the M2 QA gate ([qa/m2.md](qa/m2.md)) exercised them over `ssh` and Implemented otherwise; M3 and later rows are Planned.
